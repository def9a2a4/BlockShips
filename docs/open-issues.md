## #18 — custom ship speed/stats based on sails, engines

- **Labels:** enhancement, help wanted, more-info-needed
- **Link:** https://github.com/def9a2a4/BlockShips/issues/18
- **Comments:** 0

being worked on in #25 

I intend to add a feature where the max speed, acceleration, rotation speed, and vertical speed of ships and airships is determined dynamically from properties of the ship.

This issue thread is to gather feedback from anyone who might be interested in giving it. You can see the latest  version of the design doc here: https://github.com/def9a2a4/BlockShips/blob/main/docs/wip/TODO-stats.md


The key points are:

- "power" points are tracked to determine a ship's max speed, acceleration, rotation speed, and vertical movement speed. these stats are calculated based on the ratio between power points and the ship's total mass (not density). a few power points are granted to every ship by default, so even a wooden raft without any sails can still move (albeit slowly)
- "ship engines" can be crafted from blast furnaces (recipe TBD) and placed on a ship. when fueled, they add power
- wool and banners on a ship will count as "sails", these also give power, but max out sooner than engines
- airship density also affects vertical speed. a ship with density -5 will move up and down more quickly than a density of -1. as currently, ships with positive density are non-airships, float on water, and cannot move up and down.

I have yet to nail down many of the specifics, in particular with regards to the scaling of power/mass and its relation to stats. as a rule of thumb, a ship made entirely of wooden planks and wool blocks, with 1 wool block for every 2 planks, will move around as fast as all ships do currently. sails will max out at a 1:1 ratio of wool:wood

**def9a2a4** (2026-06-06T19:40:30Z):

> @ContainAlphanumericCharacters and @mayacel , I may have a workaround:
> 
> BlockShips uses [Shulker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker) entities as invisible collision boxes and seats for ships. Towny's mob removal timer periodically scans town chunks and removes hostile mobs, and Shulkers are on the default removal list. When the timer fires, ship collision entities get deleted, players get ejected, and the ship becomes a ghost (the visual display entities survive since they're not mobs).
> 
> **Workaround: two things to try in your Towny `config.yml`**
> 
> Under `protection`, try one or both of these:
> 
> - **Option A:** Exempt plugin-spawned entities from mob removal (Paper servers only). BlockShips entities use `SpawnReason.CUSTOM`, so adding it here exempts them while still allowing Towny to remove naturally-spawned Shulkers:
> 
> ```yaml
> town_mob_removal_ignored_spawn_causes: TRIAL_SPAWNER,CUSTOM
> ```
> 
> - **Option B:** Remove Shulker from the removal list entirely. Simpler, works on all server types, but also prevents Towny from removing naturally-spawned Shulkers within your towns (e.g. End cities):
> 
> `town_mob_removal_entities: Monster,Flying,Slime,SkeletonHorse,ZombieHorse,` ~~Shulker~~
> 
> See [this line](https://github.com/TownyAdvanced/Towny/blob/b662ff631c8f05cdf4fdbb35156eebe10b7e3e59/Towny/src/main/java/com/palmergames/bukkit/config/ConfigNodes.java#L2005) in towny config.
> 
> Reload Towny with `/townyadmin` reload after making changes.
> 
> Relevant docs:
> - [Towny config](https://github.com/TownyAdvanced/Towny/wiki/Default-Config.yml) 
> - [SpawnReason values](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/1.21.4/org/bukkit/event/entity/CreatureSpawnEvent.SpawnReason.html)
> 
> A code-side fix is in progress that will handle this automatically so server admins won't need to touch config. Will update this thread when it ships.

### Comments

**def9a2a4** (2026-03-30T05:37:47Z):

> Hi @ContainAlphanumericCharacters,
> 
> Unfortunately, Folia support would require significant modifications. The two core problems are:
> - In Folia, entities can only belong to one region at a time. A large enough ship can span multiple chunks (the code handling this is already, by far, the most complicated part of this plugin), and this becomes exponentially more complicated with Folia since we have no guarantees about what region is ticking any specific entity. in practice, since this is perhaps a rare edge case, it may be possible to simply force-disassemble a ship if at any point it is detected to be split among different threads.
> - ProtocolLib support for folia is experimental, and ProtocolLib is required for moving ships to work.
> 
> This is likely not an impossible task, just a difficult and time consuming one. I can make no guarantees of when, if ever, Folia will be supported by this plugin.
> 
> I would also direct you to the [License](https://github.com/def9a2a4/BlockShips/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) for this plugin. Usage on commercial servers, as defined by the license, is not permitted without obtaining a separate commercial license. Since Folia is generally only useful for large servers with many players, and these servers are much more likely to be commercial, it is very likely that if I do end up taking the time and effort to produce a Folia version of the plugin, that version will require a commercial license, the terms of which are decided on a case by case basis.
