### Comments

**def9a2a4** (2026-08-24T16:28:49Z):

> Hi @paulp712, this is a known limitation, but thank you for pointing it out.
> 
> The fundamental issue is that BlockShips has no way of knowing what counts as "inside" or "outside" your ship. When your ship is assembled, it only grabs the allowed blocks (not water or air), and turns those into entities. When the ship is placed back in the world, only the real blocks themselves are placed down, and thus anything clipping into a water block becomes waterlogged (an intentional feature) or simply remains water.
> 
> Solving this would require some kind of way of robustly detecting:
> - the exterior water level (tricky when the surrounding water is not level)
> - the "interior" of the ship, via some kind of floor fill below the water level, from some player-specified point
> 
> Both of the above are poorly defined, would be prone to weird edge cases, and come at a nontrivial performance cost. For now, I would consider this feature to be out of scope, but I am not opposed to pull requests which add this feature.
> 
> Please also note that the buoyancy calculation for ships is dynamic: you can make your ship float higher in the water with light blocks (glowstone and other magic light blocks are considered "lighter than air", wood and wool are both light) or heavier with blocks of iron, gold, and so on.

This is the downside of using any closed source software -- if something breaks, there is nothing you can do.
> 
> # Possible Fix
> 
> I am not completely sure this will work, but give it a shot and please let me know how it goes!
> 
> In your **MythicMobs** config, edit `plugins/MythicMobs/config-spawning.yml` and change the random-spawn generator away from the async CLUSTER path:
> 
> ```yaml
> RandomSpawning:
>   Generator: LEGACY   # was CLUSTER
> ```
> 
> `LEGACY` hopefully keeps random spawns working using the older generator that perhaps doesn't do the async chunk scan. (`NONE` disables random point-generation entirely if you don't need it.) Reload/restart after changing. See MythicMobs' [config-spawning docs](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/wikis/config/config-spawning) for the `Generator` key and [Random Spawns](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/wikis/Random-Spawns) for an overview.
> 
> 
> # Cause
> 
> MythicMobs' `CLUSTER` random-spawn generator runs on an async scheduler thread (your stack trace is on `lumine-scheduler-44`). But `Chunk.getEntities()` is a main-thread-only Bukkit API, and calling it off-thread is unsafe. When it runs while entities in that chunk are mid-update, it reads a half-updated entity list and hits a `null`, throwing the `NullPointerException`. That one exception aborts the scan cycle, so spawns freeze and `@PlayersInRadius` goes blind in those chunks until it recovers.
> 
> BlockShips doesn't create the null, it merely widens the window MythicMobs' async read races into. A ship is a lot of entities, all being repositioned on the main thread every tick if the ship is moving. Small and prefab ships have too few entities to reliably collide with the async scan; custom ships likely hit it constantly. This is the same interaction MythicMobs has with ModelEngine, which also spawns many display entities — see [#2011](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/issues/2011) (still open). It also explains why it's now silent: recent MythicMobs builds appear to swallow the error rather than fix the race, so the scan still fails but no longer logs.
> 
> This is MythicMobs' responsibility to fix properly (they've resolved a similar "off-thread `getEntities()`" class before — e.g. [#630](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/issues/630), [#1083](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/issues/1083)). If you can, please report it on their tracker referencing the ClusterGenerator issues [#2175](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/issues/2175) and [#1711](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/issues/1711), and the ModelEngine parallel [#2011](https://git.mythiccraft.io/mythiccraft/MythicMobs/-/issues/2011).

## #18 — custom ship speed/stats based on sails, engines

- **State:** open
- **Labels:** enhancement, help wanted, more-info-needed
- **Link:** https://github.com/def9a2a4/BlockShips/issues/18
- **Comments:** 1

being worked on in #25 

I intend to add a feature where the max speed, acceleration, rotation speed, and vertical speed of ships and airships is determined dynamically from properties of the ship.

This issue thread is to gather feedback from anyone who might be interested in giving it. You can see the latest  version of the design doc here: https://github.com/def9a2a4/BlockShips/blob/main/docs/wip/TODO-stats.md


The key points are:

- "power" points are tracked to determine a ship's max speed, acceleration, rotation speed, and vertical movement speed. these stats are calculated based on the ratio between power points and the ship's total mass (not density). a few power points are granted to every ship by default, so even a wooden raft without any sails can still move (albeit slowly)
- "ship engines" can be crafted from blast furnaces (recipe TBD) and placed on a ship. when fueled, they add power
- wool and banners on a ship will count as "sails", these also give power, but max out sooner than engines
- airship density also affects vertical speed. a ship with density -5 will move up and down more quickly than a density of -1. as currently, ships with positive density are non-airships, float on water, and cannot move up and down.

I have yet to nail down many of the specifics, in particular with regards to the scaling of power/mass and its relation to stats. as a rule of thumb, a ship made entirely of wooden planks and wool blocks, with 1 wool block for every 2 planks, will move around as fast as all ships do currently. sails will max out at a 1:1 ratio of wool:wood

>

> town_mob_removal_ignored_spawn_causes: TRIAL_SPAWNER,CUSTOM
> ```
> 
> - **Option B:** Remove Shulker from the removal list entirely. Simpler, works on all server types, but also prevents Towny from removing naturally-spawned Shulkers within your towns (e.g. End cities):
> 
> `town_mob_removal_entities: Monster,Flying,Slime,SkeletonHorse,ZombieHorse,` ~~Shulker~~
> 
> See [this line](https://github.com/TownyAdvanced/Towny/blob/b662ff631c8f05cdf4fdbb35156eebe10b7e3e59/Towny/src/main/java/com/palmergames/bukkit/config/ConfigNodes.java#L2005) in towny config.
> 
> Reload Towny with `/townyadmin` reload after making changes.
> 
> Relevant docs:
> - [Towny config](https://github.com/TownyAdvanced/Towny/wiki/Default-Config.yml) 
> - [SpawnReason values](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/1.21.4/org/bukkit/event/entity/CreatureSpawnEvent.SpawnReason.html)

> - In Folia, entities can only belong to one region at a time. A large enough ship can span multiple chunks (the code handling this is already, by far, the most complicated part of this plugin), and this becomes exponentially more complicated with Folia since we have no guarantees about what region is ticking any specific entity. in practice, since this is perhaps a rare edge case, it may be possible to simply force-disassemble a ship if at any point it is detected to be split among different threads.
> - ProtocolLib support for folia is experimental, and ProtocolLib is required for moving ships to work.
